Prices are subject to change without prior notice based on manufacturer revisions and applicable GST rates.

3. Installation & On-Site Services

On-site installation and maintenance services provided by our technicians are governed by the following guidelines:

  • Clients must ensure safe and uninterrupted access to work areas during installation.
  • Any pre-existing structural limitations, electrical wiring faults, or civil work required for cabling must be handled or cleared by the client.
  • Service charges estimated prior to inspection may vary if additional hardware or complex cabling routing is required on-site.

4. Warranty & Replacement Policy

All hardware products carry standard manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 3 years depending on the OEM brand (e.g., Hikvision, CP Plus, Dell, HP, eSSL). Sun Systems facilitates warranty claims directly through authorized service centers. Physical damage, liquid spills, power surge blowouts, or unauthorized tampering void all product warranties.

For clients enrolled under an Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) for computers, printers, or CCTV networks, services will be delivered in accordance with the signed SLA agreement.
