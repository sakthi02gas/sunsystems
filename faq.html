Frequently Asked Questions (30 Detailed Answers)

1. Where is Sun Systems located, and what areas do you serve?
Sun Systems is headquartered at No-7 - G1, ABC Rajya Apartment, 6th Cross St, LIC Colony, Pammal, Chennai - 600075. We serve Pammal, Pallavaram, Chromepet, Tambaram, Guindy, Velachery, and across Chennai.
2. Do you provide on-site CCTV installation for homes and offices?
Yes, we provide end-to-end HD and 4K IP CCTV camera installations for residential villas, apartments, retail showrooms, corporate offices, and factories.
3. How does remote IT support work?
Remote support allows our certified engineers to troubleshoot software errors, printer issues, and email configurations online via AnyDesk or TeamViewer.
4. What brands of laptops and computers do you repair?
We repair all major brands including Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS, Acer, Apple MacBooks, and custom desktop PCs with chip-level expertise.
5. Do you offer Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for businesses?
Yes, we offer flexible Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) covering office computers, laptops, printers, servers, and CCTV networks with scheduled preventive visits.
6. Are your hardware products original and do they carry a warranty?
All products supplied by Sun Systems are 100% brand sealed, original, and sourced directly from authorized distributors with official manufacturer warranties.
7. How can I view my CCTV cameras on my smartphone?
We configure P2P cloud applications such as Hik-Connect, gCMOB, or DMSS on your mobile phone, allowing live streaming from anywhere securely.
8. What types of biometric attendance machines do you supply?
We supply advanced eSSL, Realtime, and ZKTeco biometric terminals featuring AI facial recognition, fingerprint scanning, and automated payroll integration.
9. How can I request a free price quotation?
You can request a free quote by filling out our online form, calling our helpline at +91 80720 00902, or messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 95000 32808.
10. What are your working hours for emergency support?
Our regular office hours are Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Emergency breakdown support can be coordinated via WhatsApp on Sundays.
11. Do you undertake structured LAN cabling for new offices?
Yes, we offer professional structured Cat6/Cat6A LAN cabling, switch rack management, and high-speed Wi-Fi mesh access point deployments for new workspaces.
12. Can you upgrade my slow laptop hard disk to an SSD?
Yes, we replace slow mechanical hard drives with high-speed NVMe or SATA SSDs, boosting computer startup and application speed up to 10x.
13. Do you repair multi-function printers and refill laser toners?
Yes, we service HP, Canon, Epson, and Brother printers, fix paper jam errors, and supply high-yield original or compatible laser toner cartridges.
14. What is ColorVu technology in CCTV cameras?
ColorVu technology allows surveillance cameras to capture bright, full-color video footage even in pitch-dark nighttime conditions without infrared black-and-white mode.
15. Do you supply enterprise servers for corporate data storage?
Yes, we supply Dell PowerEdge and HP ProLiant rack and tower servers configured with RAID controllers and secure backup storage solutions.
16. How do video door phones (VDP) work for apartments?
VDP systems feature an outdoor bell camera with night vision and an indoor 7-inch color monitor, allowing residents to screen visitors and unlock gates remotely.
17. Are your field technicians certified and experienced?
Yes, all our hardware and networking technicians have years of certified field experience in security system deployments and computer troubleshooting.
18. Can I purchase computer hardware components individually?
Yes, we sell individual computer components including RAM modules, NVMe SSDs, graphics cards, motherboards, SMPS power supplies, and surveillance hard drives.
19. What payment methods do you accept for services and products?
We accept cash, bank wire transfers (NEFT/RTGS), UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and major credit/debit cards with GST invoices.
20. Do you provide wireless burglar alarm systems for retail shops?
Yes, we install wireless intruder alarm systems equipped with magnetic door sensors, motion detectors, and GSM auto-dialers for retail shop security.
21. How long does a typical CCTV installation take?
A standard residential CCTV installation (4 to 8 cameras) typically takes 4 to 6 hours, while larger commercial projects are scheduled as per site complexity.
22. Can you recover data from crashed laptop hard drives?
Yes, our technical team offers data recovery services for corrupted or physically damaged hard drives and solid-state storage media.
23. Do you provide temporary standby laptops during repairs?
For corporate clients enrolled in our AMC programs, we arrange standby workstations or laptops while major repairs are being completed in our lab.
24. What is the advantage of PoE switches in CCTV networks?
PoE (Power over Ethernet) switches transmit both electrical power and video data through a single Ethernet cable, eliminating the need for individual power sockets near cameras.
25. How often should office computers undergo preventive maintenance?
We recommend professional cleaning, antivirus updates, and system optimization checks at least once every three months to prevent sudden hardware failures.
26. Do you handle optical fiber cabling for long-distance networks?
Yes, we undertake armored optical fiber cable laying, fusion splicing, and media converter setups for college campuses and large manufacturing plants.
27. Can biometric attendance software integrate with payroll systems?
Yes, the biometric attendance software we install generates automated Excel and database exports compatible with standard payroll accounting software.
28. Do you provide on-site diagnosis for desktop power failure issues?
Yes, our engineers visit your location to diagnose SMPS power supply failures, motherboard shorts, or RAM faults and resolve them promptly.
29. How can I submit feedback or a testimonial about your service?
You can easily share your experience by visiting our "Testimonials" page and filling out the instant feedback form, which updates our review grid immediately.
